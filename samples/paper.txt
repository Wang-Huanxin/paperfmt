2. * The GCD or Greatest Common Divisor of two positive integers is the greatest 
integer that divides evenly into them both. For example GCD(12,18)=6, because 
12 and 18 are both divisible by 6 and no larger number. 
 
The GCD can be computed using the “Euclidean algorithm”, which is at least 
2300 years old. 
 
Wikipedia describes a recursive implementation of this algorithm at 
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euclidean_algorithm. 
 
Implement this recursive algorithm in C++ and use it to calculate 
GCD(45276,14994) along with the number of function calls used.
